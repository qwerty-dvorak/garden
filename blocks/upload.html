Three of the backgrounds read a picture instead of a formula. image shows it, ascii redraws it out of the charset, and mask uses it as a stencil that text and motion are only allowed to appear inside. All three take the picture from a slot, and there are five slots.

Nothing here is uploaded. The word is wrong and kept only because it is the word people look for. A picture you choose is decoded in this tab and written to IndexedDB on your own machine; no request carries it anywhere, and the server has no route that would accept one if it tried.

the two that ship

Two pictures already in the site are offered as samples, so there is something to point the programs at without going and finding a file. life is a frame of Conway's life — high contrast, mostly empty, which is what makes it a good stencil. phyllotaxis is a vector spiral, so it is all gentle gradient and no hard edge, which is the opposite problem and useful for exactly that reason.

A slot with nothing in it falls back to the first of those rather than to nothing, which is why the playground shows the picture programs doing something instead of two blank rectangles. Put your own picture in a slot and that stops.

what to expect

ascii   wants contrast. a flat photograph turns to mud —
        push `ascii contrast` up until the shapes separate.
mask    wants a subject. it draws only where the picture is
        bright, so a dark photograph masks almost everything
        away. invert it, or drop the threshold.

The knobs are on their own page: which slot, how hard to read it, what text to pour into the stencil. The playground shows every program at once and is the fastest way to see whether a picture is worth keeping.
