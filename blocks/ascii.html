Every program registered on the engine, running at once. Each panel is its own runner on its own grid; they pause the moment they leave the viewport, which is the only reason fourteen of them at the same time is a reasonable thing to do.

Click a panel to make it the background of every page here. The knobs are on their own page, and the writeup explains what the engine is and where it came from.

The numbers on the right of each caption are its grid size and its measured frame rate.

what other people left

Backgrounds people have shared, newest first. A hundred at most — past that the oldest one falls off the end. Anything you post yourself gets a delete button, because the server hands your browser a token when it accepts the entry and keeps only a hash of it: it cannot tell who you are, and does not need to, since the only question a delete button has to answer is whether this is the browser that posted it.

Dials only — a program name, a charset, one line of k=v. Pictures never travel, so a shared ascii or mask draws against whatever is in your own slots. Put something in them here.

the ramps

A cell holds an index, not a character, so the alphabet is a separate decision from the program. These are the ones that ship. Any string works — dark on the left, light on the right.

standard   .:-=+*#%@
long       .'`^",:;Il!i><~+_-?][}{1)(|\/tfjrxnuvczXYUJCLQ0OZmwqpdbkhao*#MW&8%B@$
block      ░▒▓█
grade      ▁▂▃▄▅▆▇█
dots       ·∙•●
line       ╴╵└╶─┌┬╷┘│┤┴┼
arrow      ↑↗→↘↓↙←↖
stroke     /\|-_+*XY#
binary     01

Yes, < and & are legal in a ramp. They are glyphs. Every glyph is escaped once when the ramp is loaded, into a parallel array the markup path indexes instead — so a ramp of  <&> draws angle brackets rather than breaking out of the document.
